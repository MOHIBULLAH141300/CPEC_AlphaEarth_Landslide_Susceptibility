# Temporal Summary Outputs 2017-2024

These products summarize annual fused susceptibility maps from the fixed 2018 V3 Spatial-CV Stacked Ensemble. They should be described as temporal-transfer / dynamic-covariate stress-test outputs, not as eight independently validated annual models.

High-probability threshold: 2018 fused P80 = `0.126336`.

Persistent high susceptibility: probability >= P80 in at least 6 of 8 years.

Main rasters:
- mean probability
- temporal standard deviation
- temporal range
- Theil-Sen trend slope per year
- high-probability year count and fraction
- persistent high-probability mask
- year of maximum and minimum probability
- consecutive year-to-year change maps
